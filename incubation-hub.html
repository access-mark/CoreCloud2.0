Incubation Hub

We’re building South Africa’s next wave of cloud, data, and platform companies. Our QSE Accelerator is a pay-it-forward initiative — practical operator help, tooling credits, and market access that compound into jobs, exports, and sustainable revenue.

Explore the QSE Collective Partner / Sponsor Apply (Founder)

Why We’re Doing This

Pay-it-forward responsibility: we’ve benefitted from the SA tech community. The Hub returns that by lowering the activation energy for credible founders and QSEs.

Execution over theatre: less pitch-deck coaching, more platform builds, governance guardrails, and sales motions that actually convert.

National outcomes: local jobs, export revenue, and operator capability that stays in the ecosystem.

How It Works

Operator Clinics

Weekly sessions on landing zones, security baselines, FinOps hygiene, SLOs, data platforms, and GTM mechanics. Real artefacts: IaC, runbooks, dashboards.

Tooling & Credits

Curated stack with credits and sensible defaults: cloud, CI/CD, observability, ticketing, docs. Guardrails baked in to avoid surprise spend.

Market Access

Introductions to buyers and partners, co-sell opportunities, and “first logo” pathways — with referenceable outcomes and proof-of-value framing.

QSE Accelerator (Pay-It-Forward)

Who: South African Qualifying Small Enterprises and early-stage startups in cloud, data, AI, platform, or security services/products.

What we provide: technical baselines (AWS/Azure/hosted), reference architectures, DevSecOps pipelines, FinOps discipline, bid support, and leadership coaching.

How we fund it: CoreCloud time, partner credits, and corporate sponsors who view this as real corporate responsibility — not a line-item donation.

Corporate Responsibility, With Evidence

Transparent metrics: published dashboards on jobs created, revenue enabled, exports, certifications achieved, and R&D velocity.

Audit-ready artefacts: SOWs, change logs, attendance, and value tracking per cohort — suitable for ESG/CSI reporting.

Local first: preference for SA-resident data and suppliers where practical; POPIA-aware designs by default.

Cohort Flow (12 Weeks)

Weeks 1–3: Baseline

Identity & access, network guards, IaC repo, CI/CD, observability hooks, cost controls, and basic sales instrumentation.

Weeks 4–8: Ship

Thin-slice value to a real customer. PoV framing, SLOs, backup/DR tests, and security evidence packs.

Weeks 9–12: Scale

Pricing & packaging, partner motions, case studies, and a governance rhythm that holds under growth.

FAQ

What stacks? AWS, Azure, or hosted private cloud — with data, AI, and security guardrails.
