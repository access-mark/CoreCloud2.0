Identity-first guardrails for AWS & Azure. Reduce blast radius, protect data, and keep developers fast.

Business Outcomes

  • Smaller blast radius, fewer critical incidents.
  • Audit-ready with evidence mapped to POPIA / ISO / CIS.
  • Delivery speed preserved — controls embedded in pipelines.
  • Clear ownership: who approves, who operates, who audits.

Operator Rhythm

Diagnose → Design → Do → Drive. We land the baseline in weeks, then run posture reviews and drift response monthly.

Controls Framework

  • Org guardrails (AWS Orgs / Azure Policy), SCPs/deny-by-default, enforced MFA.
  • Federated SSO, least-privilege roles; right-sizing jobs; break-glass with rotation + logging.
  • Zero-trust segmentation, private endpoints, WAF/DDoS, egress control.
  • Service-to-service auth (mTLS/OIDC); secrets via KMS/Key Vault.
  • Encryption in transit/at rest; key rotation & lifecycle; envelope encryption for high-risk data.
  • Classification + residency policies aligned to POPIA.
  • Hardened images, EDR, container/IaC scanning in CI; signed artifacts; drift detection.
  • CloudTrail/Activity Logs → SIEM; detective controls; evidence mapped to CIS/NIST/ISO/POPIA.
  • Quarterly posture reviews with exec reporting.
  • IR playbooks per service; ring-fenced forensics; immutable backups; tabletop exercises.
