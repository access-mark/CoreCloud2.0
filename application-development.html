Application Development — Ship Fast, Stay Governed

What We Build

  • Internal apps & workflows: approvals, SLAs, integrations, and auditable trails.
  • Customer-facing products: APIs, portals, mobile/web front-ends with secure identity.
  • Event-driven backends: queues/streams, idempotent processors, and durable outboxes.
  • Data-aware services: contracts, lineage, and privacy patterns by data class.
  • Hybrid patterns: hosted/private cloud primaries with burst to AWS/Azure.

When low-code fits, we use it for speed — with domain services in APIs so you can swap UIs later. See: Low-Code, High Impact.

How We Ship (Without Rework)

  • Two-tier architecture: thin UI + stable domain services; API-first with versioning.
  • Quality gates: automated tests, contracts, CI/CD with blue/green or feature flags.
  • Security by default: SSO, least-privilege IAM, secrets management, audit logs.
  • Observability: structured logs, metrics, traces, SLOs, and error budgets.
  • Change safety: rollbacks and migrations planned before the first story starts.

30-Day MVP (Production-Quality)

  1. Week 1: journeys, golden paths, DOR/DOD, metrics of “done”.
  2. Week 2: scaffolding, identity, error handling, and two end-to-end flows in sandbox.
  3. Week 3: logging, audit, access control, and UAT with 10–20 users.
  4. Week 4: load test hotspots, runbook/rollback, go-live.

Guardrails That Don’t Slow You Down

  • Identity & access: SSO/OIDC, least-privilege roles, break-glass procedures.
  • Secrets & keys: vault/KMS, rotation, and sealed pipelines.
  • Compliance: POPIA data classes, residency, and revocation logs.
  • Environments: dev/test/stage/prod with promotion controls and audit.
  • Versioned configs: IaC and app config in git; change reviews and drift detection.

Data & AI — Built In, Not Bolted On

  • Contracts & lineage: schemas, PII tagging, and reproducible pipelines.
  • Lakehouse integration: Databricks patterns for batch + streaming.
  • RAG & ML workflows: evaluated, monitored, and deployed where latency wins.
  • Analytics exits: operational data exported to your warehouse with privacy preserved.

See also: Data & AI for platform choices and governance.

Cost & Governance (By Design)

  • Tagging & budgets: coverage targets, alerts, and dashboards.
  • Right-sizing: lifecycle policies, scale-to-zero, and commitments where it pays.
  • Showback: costs mapped to teams/features to drive better decisions.
