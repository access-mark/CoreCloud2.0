Global Talent

Elite South African engineers for global teams — vetted, timezone-aligned, cost-efficient. We place cloud, data, platform, and app talent into UK/EU/MEA/US organisations with practical oversight and a bias for execution.

Why South Africa for Global Placements?

Execution under pressure: resilient, outcome-driven professionals who deliver without hand-holding — comfortable with constraints and ambiguity.

Timezone advantage: real-time collaboration with UK/EU/MEA; workable overlap with US East; US West by arrangement.

Global skill, better economics: senior capability at ~40–60% lower cost than UK/EU/US equivalents, without compromising standards.

Cultural fit: pragmatic, straight-talking, client-oriented — easy integration into international teams.

What We Place

Cloud & Platform: AWS/Azure engineers, landing zones, IaC, platform ops, SRE/DevOps.

Data & AI: data engineers, analytics engineers, ML engineers, MLOps/Databricks.

Application Delivery: cloud-native, mobile, and web — built for speed, governance, and scale.

Leadership & Architecture: solution/enterprise architects, delivery leads, FinOps & governance specialists.

Engagement Models

Contract placements (3–12 months): extend or convert as needs evolve.

Delivery pods (2–5 people): outcome-focused squads with CoreCloud oversight.

White-label partnering: we supply vetted engineers to MSPs/SIs under your brand.

We handle vetting, references, timezone fit, onboarding. Payroll/EOR available via partner on request.

Assurance & Compliance

Evidence over claims: delivery stories, referenceable outcomes, and environment specifics — not keyword CVs.

Scenario-based screening: architecture decisions, incident narratives, or CI/CD/practices — clarity and judgement under pressure.

Data residency & sovereignty: we can keep data resident in SA while serving global teams and complying with client policies.

Replacement guarantee: if a placement fails in probation, we replace — same fee, no risk.
