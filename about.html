About CoreCloud

We help South African organisations modernise—cloud, data & AI, FinOps, and application delivery— with residency, governance, and cost-right design from day one. Our work is outcome-led, practical, and grounded in the realities of SA infrastructure, compliance, and budget.

Our Mission

Unlock measurable value from cloud and data—faster, safer, and more predictably—while building internal capability that lasts. We focus on thin-slice delivery, clear SLOs, and cost guardrails so progress compounds without surprises.

What We Do

  • Cloud Foundations:Landing zones, identity, IaC, and hybrid patterns that stand up in production.
  • Data & AI:Lakehouse, streaming, governance, and pragmatic AI (RAG/MLOps) with SA residency.
  • App Delivery:Cloud-native services, portals, mobile/web—shipped with DevOps and security by design.
  • FinOps & Governance:Budgets, golden tags, policy-as-code, and dashboards that finance trusts.
  • Hosting & On-Prem:SA-hosted workloads, on-prem GPU for steady inference, cloud for burst.
  • Talent & Enablement:Re/Launch pathways, embedded squads, runbooks, and skills that stick.

Principles We Work By

  • Outcome first:Define the decision, metric, and owner before we build anything.
  • Thin-slice delivery:Ship value in weeks; reduce risk while building momentum.
  • Security & cost by default:Guardrails and budgets live in code; drift gets surfaced fast.
  • Operate what we build:Runbooks, SLOs, and on-call posture from day one—no throw-overs.

Footprint & Partners

We deliver across South Africa with a partner collective for specialised needs—GPU hosting, security tooling, and industry platforms—so you get the right solution without vendor lock-in. Our posture is multi-venue by design to preserve your leverage.

Leadership

CoreCloud is led by practitioners who’ve built and operated platforms at scale. We value clarity, bias to action, and durable standards—because enterprise change only sticks when it works in production and on the balance sheet.

Careers at CoreCloud

  • Practitioner DNA:Engineers, SREs, architects, product leads—people who ship.
  • Learning loop:Labs, brown-bags, and certification support with real-world projects.
  • Flexible models:Permanent, consulting, and squad augmentation pathways.
