About CoreCloud

We help South African organisations modernise—cloud, data & AI, FinOps, and app delivery—with governance and cost-right design from day one.

Our Mission

Unlock measurable value from cloud and data—faster, safer, and more predictably—while building internal capability that lasts.

What We Do

  • Cloud Foundations:Landing zones, identity, IaC, and hybrid patterns that stand up in production.
  • Data & AI:Lakehouse, streaming, governance, and pragmatic AI with SA residency.
  • App Delivery:Cloud-native services, portals, mobile/web—shipped with DevOps and security by design.
  • FinOps & Governance:Budgets, golden tags, and dashboards that finance trusts.
  • Hosting & On-Prem:SA-hosted workloads, on-prem GPU for steady inference, cloud for burst.
  • Talent & Enablement:Re/Launch pathways, embedded squads, and skills that stick.

Principles We Work By

  • Outcome first:Define the decision, metric, and owner before we build anything.
  • Thin-slice delivery:Ship value in weeks; reduce risk while building momentum.
  • Security & cost by default:Guardrails and budgets live in code.

Leadership

CoreCloud is led by practitioners who’ve built and operated platforms at scale. We value clarity, bias to action, and durable standards.
