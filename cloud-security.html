Controls Framework

  • Org guardrails (AWS Orgs / Azure Policy), SCPs/deny-by-default, enforced MFA.
  • Federated SSO; least-privilege roles; automated right-sizing; break-glass with rotation & logs.
  • Zero-trust segmentation, private endpoints, WAF/DDoS, strict egress.
  • Service-to-service auth (mTLS/OIDC); secrets via KMS/Key Vault.
  • Encryption in transit/at rest; key lifecycle & rotation; envelope encryption for high-risk data.
  • Classification & residency policies aligned to POPIA.
  • Hardened images; EDR; container/IaC scanning in CI; signed artifacts; drift detection.
  • CloudTrail/Activity Logs → SIEM; detective controls; evidence mapped to CIS/NIST/ISO/POPIA.
  • Quarterly posture reviews with exec reporting.
  • IR playbooks per service; ring-fenced forensics; immutable backups; tabletop exercises.
