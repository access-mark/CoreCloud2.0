Outcome over theory

Controls that stick under load — least privilege, clean boundaries, and automated checks that don’t break delivery.

Built for hybrid

Cloud where it wins; hosted/on-prem where it must. Policies and telemetry align across venues.

  • Org guardrails (AWS Organizations / Azure Policy), SCPs/deny-by-default, and enforced MFA.
  • Federated SSO, least-privilege roles with automated right-sizing; break-glass with rotation & logs.
  • Zero-trust segmentation, private endpoints, WAF/DDoS, and tight egress control.
  • Service-to-service auth (mTLS/OIDC); secrets via native managers.
  • Encryption at rest/in transit; KMS/Key Vault lifecycle & rotation; envelope encryption for high-risk data.
  • Classification mapped to POPIA; residency enforced by policy.
  • Hardened images, EDR, container/IaC scanning in CI; signed artifacts and drift detection.
  • CloudTrail/Activity Logs to SIEM, control evidence mapped to CIS/NIST/ISO/POPIA.
  • Automated evidence packs; quarterly posture reviews.
  • IR playbooks per service, ring-fenced forensics, immutable backups, tabletop exercises.
