Sovereign Data & AI

AI that respects SA residency and cost reality. We build governed data platforms and AI that run where they should — on-prem GPU for steady load, cloud for burst — with lineage, controls, and speed.

Why Hybrid for Data & AI (SA Reality)

  • Residency + Reach:Keep sensitive data in SA while bursting to global regions for training or scale.
  • Latency + Jitter:Inference and BI close to users and events; minimise cross-border hops.
  • Cost Control:Steady workloads on predictable on-prem/hosted; cloud for true variability only.
  • Vendor Leverage:Multi-venue posture improves pricing power and avoids lock-in.

Reference Architecture (Thin but Complete)

  • Ingest:Batch + streaming (files, DB change capture, events, APIs). Contracts define schema, SLAs, and ownership.
  • Lakehouse:Bronze (raw), Silver (validated), Gold (curated data products). ACID tables, quality checks, and governance hooks.
  • Processing:ETL/ELT jobs, feature engineering, and vectorisation pipelines with lineage stamped per run.
  • Serving:BI & metrics, feature store, vector DB, model endpoints, and semantic layers.
  • MLOps / LLMOps:Model registry, evaluation, A/B, rollout, drift watch, and rollback with audit.
  • Security & Identity:Single sign-on, least privilege, attribute-based access, masking, key management.
  • Observability:Data quality, freshness, pipeline reliability, cost & performance dashboards.
  • FinOps:Budgets, alerts, showback/chargeback by product/team; GPU utilisation & SP/RI coverage.

Operating Model

  • Discover:Prioritised use-cases with ROI, data audit, residency constraints, and success metrics.
  • Deliver:Thin slice first; ship data product + model + dashboard in weeks, not months.
  • Govern:Contracts, lineage, approvals, and policy-as-code for privacy and retention.
  • Operate:SLOs, on-call, anomaly watch, cost/perf reviews, and quarterly roadmap refresh.

AI Patterns We Ship

  • RAG Assistants:Document Q&A, agent assist, knowledge retrieval with source grounding and redaction.
  • Fine-Tuning:Domain-specific optimisation for style, safety, or task accuracy; evaluation gates before rollout.
  • Batch Inference:Periodic scoring for churn/risk/propensity with lineage and QA snapshots.
  • Streaming Inference:Low-latency features and decisions (fraud, personalisation) with jitter budgets.

Governance & Risk Controls

  • POPIA by Design:Purpose limitation, minimisation, consent, and retention managed as code.
  • Access & Masking:Role/attribute-based policies, tokenisation, and dynamic masking in serving layers.
  • Lineage & Approvals:End-to-end lineage; gated promotion with risk checks and audit trails.
  • Egress Controls:Residency guardrails; only approved cross-border routes with monitoring.

Performance & Cost Engineering

  • GPU Efficiency:Right-sizing, batching, quantisation, and utilisation targets with alerts.
  • Storage Strategy:Hot/warm/cold tiering, caching, compaction, and egress-aware placement.
  • Autoscale & SLOs:Scale to latency/error budgets; pre-warm for peak; fallbacks defined.
  • FinOps Guardrails:Budgets, anomaly detection, showback by team, and monthly variance reviews.

KPIs We Track

  • Time to First Value:Days/weeks to first shipped use-case with measurable ROI.
  • Data Freshness:% meeting SLA; late pipeline alarms with root-cause notes.
  • Model Quality:Eval scores, human-in-the-loop acceptance, hallucination/guardrail metrics.
  • Cost per Outcome:Cost per inference/query/dataset; GPU utilisation; SP/RI coverage.
