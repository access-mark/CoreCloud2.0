Low-Code, High Impact

Apps • 25 Sep 2025 • 5-minute read

Low-code promises speed, but not every workload belongs there. Here’s how SA teams decide without burning cycles.

Use low-code when

  • Prototypes: validate business logic in weeks, not months.
  • Internal tools: forms, workflows, dashboards with limited external users.
  • Short lifecycle apps: campaign landing pages, pilot portals.

Go custom when

  • Scale matters: thousands of concurrent users, heavy integrations.
  • Core IP: what differentiates you can’t live in a generic builder.
  • Complex rules: multiple data sources, compliance controls.
