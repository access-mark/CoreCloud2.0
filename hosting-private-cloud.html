Hosting & Private Cloud

Not every workload belongs in hyperscale. We engineer the right venue—Hosted Private Cloud, On-Prem, or Hybrid—so you cut cost, hit latency targets, and meet SA compliance without losing agility.

Why companies are moving off pure hyperscale

Predictable cost

Steady ERP, DB, and integration workloads often run cheaper hosted or on-prem than in elastic hyperscale.

Locality & compliance

Keep data in South Africa to simplify POPIA and industry rules. Minimise cross-region egress and surprises.

Latency & reliability

Lower jitter for trading/analytics. Clear SLAs, backup, and DR with real accountability.

How we choose the best venue

Public Cloud

Elastic and bursty, modern services, AI/ML, global reach. Use when elasticity or managed services win.

Hosted Private Cloud

Local DCs, predictable cost, SA data locality, bundled DR. Ideal for steady line-of-business systems.

On-Prem Private Cloud

Dell VxRail, HPE GreenLake, Nutanix AHV. Subscription consumption, full control, modern automation.

Hybrid by design

Keep cloud where it wins; host the rest. Unified landing zone, identity, guardrails, and observability.

FAQ

Will we lose cloud agility?

No. We keep burst/innovation in AWS/Azure where it wins and place steady workloads on hosted/on-prem for cost and control.

What about vendor lock-in?

We’re vendor-neutral. Designs are portable across Dell, HPE, Nutanix and major SA DC providers.

How fast can we repatriate?

Quick wins within weeks. We prioritise high-cost, low-elasticity workloads first to fund the rest.

Do you handle operations?

Yes. SLA-backed managed services with monitoring, backup/DR, patching, and compliance reviews.
