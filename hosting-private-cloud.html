Why companies are moving off pure hyperscale

  • Total cost you can predict: ERP, databases, integration hubs often run cheaper hosted/on-prem.
  • Locality & compliance: Keep data in SA, simplify POPIA, reduce egress.
  • Latency & reliability: Lower jitter for trading/analytics; clear SLAs with DR.

FAQ

Will we lose cloud agility?

No. We keep burst/innovation in AWS/Azure where it wins and place steady workloads on hosted/on-prem for cost and control.

What about vendor lock-in?

We’re vendor-neutral. Designs are portable across Dell, HPE, Nutanix and major SA DC providers.

How fast can we repatriate?

Quick wins within weeks. We prioritise high-cost, low-elasticity workloads first to fund the rest.

Do you handle operations?

Yes. Managed services with local SLAs, monitoring, backup/DR, and monthly reviews.
