Hybrid: The Only Way to Balance Cost, Speed & Control

Put steady workloads where cost is predictable, keep agility where it counts, and prove compliance and resilience across venues.

Why Hybrid Works

Cost that stays flat:Steady workloads don’t need burst pricing. Host or on-prem for predictable monthly spend; keep cloud for what must scale.

Performance where it matters:Place latency-sensitive systems close to users and data. Use cloud for global reach, ML, and managed services.

Compliance by design:Keep regulated data resident in SA. Reduce cross-border egress and audit friction while retaining cloud agility.

Resilience you can prove:Clear SLAs, backup and DR across venues. Test failover without betting on one provider.

Vendor leverage:A multi-venue posture improves pricing power and avoids single-stack lock-in.

Built for South Africa

POPIA & industry rules:Keep regulated data in SA; minimise cross-border egress and audit friction.

Low jitter, local reach:Place latency-sensitive systems close to customers, branches, and trading venues.

Power & resilience:SLA-bound power redundancy and DR tested in SA data centres you can visit.

Guardrails, FinOps & Observability

Unified identity:Single sign-on and consistent roles across cloud and hosted/on-prem estates.

Policy as code:Landing zones and guardrails that stop drift before it starts.

Clear cost lines:Per-workload cost views and alerts; no more blended “mystery” bills.

Real SLOs:SLIs/SLOs tracked across venues; DR rehearsed, documented, and provable.

Why Companies Are Moving Off Pure Hyperscale

Predictable cost:Steady ERP, DB, and integration workloads often run cheaper hosted or on-prem than in elastic hyperscale.

Locality & compliance:Data residency in SA simplifies POPIA and industry rules; minimise cross-region egress.

Latency & reliability:Lower jitter for trading/analytics. Clear SLAs, backup, and DR with real accountability.

FAQ

Do we lose cloud agility?No. Keep managed services and burst in cloud; place steady or sensitive workloads locally.

How do you prove residency?Data classification, residency tags, access boundaries, and logs that back it up.

What about power risk?SLA-bound facilities with backup; DR rehearsed under realistic failover and load-shedding scenarios.

Can we phase it?Yes. Start with a thin slice: one workload, guardrails, and dashboards—then scale.
