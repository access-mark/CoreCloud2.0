RE/LAUNCH — Return to Tech with Confidence

Ready to re-enter or pivot in ICT? RE/LAUNCH is a focused program that rebuilds your positioning, story, and market assets — and gets you in front of the right opportunities faster.

Launch Relaunch

Who It’s For

Experienced professionals returning: coming back after a career break, relocation, or sabbatical — with solid delivery behind you and the appetite to lead again.

Pivoters with depth: strong adjacent skills (platform, engineering, data, PMO, security) moving into modern cloud, SRE/DevOps, data & AI, or product leadership.

Leaders levelling up: you’re ready to own bigger outcomes — influence, teams, budgets — and want a sharper story plus targeted market access.

What You Get

Positioning & story that lands: we shape your narrative around outcomes — cost, reliability, speed, risk — with evidence that survives hard questions.

CV, LinkedIn & portfolio refresh: concise, outcome-led materials with a small, sharp portfolio (architecture views, runbooks, before/after metrics, or code samples as appropriate).

Interview readiness: mock sessions that focus on trade-offs, incident narratives, and pragmatic leadership signals. You’ll walk in calm and credible.

Skills refresh plan: laser-focused refresh aligned to your target role (platform, SRE, data/AI, engineering, governance/FinOps) — thin slice, real value.

Introductions & opportunities: access to CoreCloud benches, partner collectives, and client needs where your profile fits.

How It Works

1) Calibration

We map where you’ve been and where you’re going. Role targets, market fit, and evidence we can stand behind.

2) Build

We craft your story, update assets, and prep for interviews. One thin-slice deliverable to showcase current capability.

3) Market

Targeted outreach and introductions. You interview with confidence; we iterate quickly on feedback.
