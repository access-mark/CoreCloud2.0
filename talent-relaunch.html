Re / Launch Talent Hub

Whether you’re starting out or starting over, CoreCloud is committed to helping South Africans relaunch their careers with purpose. We stand behind the overlooked, the underestimated, and the experienced.

Why We Built This

South Africa’s job market leaves too many talented people behind. Young professionals get overlooked for lack of experience. Skilled professionals are passed over for being “too expensive” or “overqualified.” CoreCloud is changing that.

Re / Launch is more than a support page. It’s a platform to help you reclaim your career. We don’t do pity placements. We focus on purpose, partnership, and performance.
