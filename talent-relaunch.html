Talent Re/Launch

A coached, employer-backed pathway for experienced professionals returning to tech, or pivoting into modern cloud, data, DevOps, and product roles. We rebuild confidence, update skills, and place you where you can win.

Apply to Re/Launch Hire from Re/Launch

Who Re/Launch Is For

  • Returners:You’ve taken time out for family, study, business, or health—and you’re ready to come back strong.
  • Pivoters:You’re proven in adjacent disciplines and want to move into cloud, platform, data/AI, DevOps, or product.
  • Immigration & relocation:Experienced professionals new to SA who need a fast, credible entry point.
  • Leaders retooling:Architects/PMs/Leads modernising their stack and operating model.

Pathways & Tracks

  • Cloud & Platform:AWS/Azure fundamentals, landing zones, IaC, Kubernetes, observability.
  • DevOps & SRE:CI/CD, pipelines, reliability, performance, cost guardrails.
  • Software Engineering:Modern backend/frontend patterns, APIs, secure-by-design.
  • Data & AI:Lakehouse, ELT/dbt, notebooks, RAG/MLOps, evaluation & drift.
  • Security & Governance:Identity, policy-as-code, standards, audit readiness (POPIA).
  • Product & Delivery:Outcome-first, thin-slice shipping, measurement, stakeholder alignment.

How the Program Works

  • 6–12 weeks coached:Blended learning + practical labs mapped to your target role.
  • Portfolio & Projects:Real artifacts (IaC repos, services, dashboards, runbooks) reviewed by hiring managers.
  • Mentors & Mock Panels:Interview calibration, whiteboard practice, scenario walkthroughs.
  • Placement Support:Warm intros to employers, scorecard-based matching, offer coaching.
  • Post-Placement:30/60/90 follow-ups to ensure ramp, impact, and retention.

Outcomes You Can Expect

  • Confidence & clarity:Current skills, current language, and a crisp narrative for interviews.
  • Job-ready artifacts:Code, pipelines, dashboards, and runbooks you can show and explain.
  • Faster placement:Shortlist to interview in days, not months, via our employer network.
  • Stickiness:First 90-day plan and regular coaching to secure early wins.

For Employers & Hiring Managers

  • Less risk, more signal:Candidates show live artifacts and role-aligned mini-projects, not just CVs.
  • Faster ramp:Re/Launch embeds your standards—tagging, guardrails, SLOs—before day 1.
  • Inclusive pipeline:Tap returners and pivoters with proven professional maturity.
  • Flexible models:Direct hire, contract-to-perm, or squad augmentation.
