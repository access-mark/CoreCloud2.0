Eliminating Waste: The Fastest ROI in Cloud

Cloud • 25 Sep 2025 • 6-minute read

Cloud should compound value, not cost. In South Africa, the fastest way to prove cloud’s worth is to cut waste without slowing delivery. Here’s the practical 30-day playbook we run with clients.

1) Find the leaks

  • Idle & orphaned: unattached volumes, stale snapshots, zombie NICs, test clusters left running.
  • Over-sized: memory-heavy instances for CPU workloads, premium storage where standard works.
  • Duplication: overlapping backups, double-buffered data, multi-region where you don’t need it.

2) Plug quick wins (week 1)

  • Turn on schedules for non-prod (stop at 19:00, start at 07:00; weekends off).
  • Set lifetime policies on snapshots & logs (30–60 days unless regulated).
  • Kill anything without a business owner tag after a 72-hour hold.

3) Right-size the big hitters

Baseline CPU, memory, IO and drop to the smallest class that sustains your 95th percentile. Swap GP3/standard SSD where IO allows. Consider savings plans/reserved where the workload is steady > 6–12 months.

4) Kill invisible idle

Databases and analytics stacks love to idle. Use auto-pause (where supported), scale-to-zero for dev, and enforce query timeouts. On Databricks, prefer jobs clusters, set termination to 10–15 min, and block interactive all-day clusters.

5) Guardrails so it sticks

  • Budget alerts with Slack/Teams routing.
  • Tag policy (env, owner, cost-center, app) enforced at create.
  • Change rhythm: weekly 30-min FinOps review; monthly commitment check.
