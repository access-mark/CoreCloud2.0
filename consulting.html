Our Advisory Approach

No buzzwords. An operator’s rhythm: Diagnose → Design → Do → Drive.

Diagnose

Start with data: bills, pipeline, SLAs, SLOs, latency maps, roles & responsibilities, and partner performance. Identify friction and the smallest move that unlocks the most value.

Design

Venue-neutral options with clear trade-offs, and a GTM/process reset aligned to how your customers actually buy. Decision logs and ready-to-run playbooks.

Do

Phase by value and risk. Self-funding quick wins first (high cost, low elasticity). Blue/green and feature flags for low-risk cutovers.

Drive

Monthly cost/perf reviews, change windows that respect the business calendar, and SRE/FinOps guardrails so improvements stick.
