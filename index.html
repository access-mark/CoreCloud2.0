Our Focus & Method

Why

SA enterprises are paying for elasticity they don’t use, running platforms that drift from standards, and missing revenue because GTM and delivery aren’t connected. We remove that friction.

What

We align four engines to a single outcome. GTM execution that sharpens pipeline, partner motions, and win-rates. Platform modernisation across AWS/Azure, hosted private cloud, and on-prem so each workload runs where it wins. Talent that stabilises ops and accelerates delivery. Applications delivered with discipline — secure, observable, and maintainable from day one.

How

We run an operator’s rhythm: Diagnose → Design → Do → Drive. Diagnose with data (bills, telemetry, SLAs). Design venue-neutral options with clear trade-offs. Do the highest-value moves first to self-fund the rest. Drive with governance — monthly cost/perf reviews, decision logs, and change windows that respect the business calendar.

Principles

Workload first. No platform bias. Guardrails over gates. Teams move fast within policy. Measure everything. Revenue lift, run-rate down, latency, reliability, and dev lead-time. Make value obvious. Before/after, trade-offs, and next best move — executive-ready.

Outcomes

Typical results: 20–40% run-rate reduction on steady workloads, double-digit latency improvements for data/analytics, faster cycle time for change, and fewer incidents because ops is designed — not inherited.

The Venue Decision Framework

Discover

Baseline cost, performance, risk, and data gravity per workload: usage patterns, seasonality, egress, storage tiers, licensing, network paths, DR posture, and POPIA zones. Map dependencies so moves don’t break upstream processes or SLAs.

Model

Compare Cloud vs Hosted vs On-Prem over 12/36 months. Include burst economics, commitments, co-lo fees, power, support, and people time. Add latency maps, DR patterns (RPO/RTO), and security controls so the cheapest option isn’t the riskiest.

Decide

Rank each workload on five axes: cost, latency, reliability/DR, compliance, change velocity. Pick self-funding quick wins first (high cost, low elasticity), sequence by dependencies, and publish a migration playbook with clear owners and dates.

Operate

Standardise landing zones across venues: identity, networking, tagging, budgets, and observability. Run FinOps as a cadence (showback, anomaly flags, rightsizing) and SRE practices to protect SLOs. Monthly reviews track run-rate, latency, incidents, and the next move.

You get

An executive deck with options and trade-offs, a per-workload venue decision with 12/36-month TCO, a phased migration plan with risk controls, and an operating model (guardrails, dashboards, review rhythm) that keeps hybrid fast and under control.
