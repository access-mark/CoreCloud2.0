Put the employee experience first to unlock productivity, engagement and collaboration — and help win the war for talent.

What “Modern” Looks Like

Device Management

Autopilot/Intune or equivalent for zero-touch build, policy, app delivery, and health.

Identity & Access

Entra ID/Okta, SSO, conditional access, least-privilege roles, break-glass controls.

Collaboration

Teams/Zoom/Webex with meeting rooms, call routing, and interop guardrails.

Security

Defender/XDR, email security, DLP and insider risk with SOC workflows.

Data Protection

3-2-1 for M365/Google Workspace, legal hold, retention, and recovery testing.

AI at Work

Copilot enablement with data boundaries, prompts training, and usage guardrails.

Outcomes We Drive

  • Happier users: first-call resolution and clear SLAs.
  • Less noise: hardened baseline reduces incidents and drift.
  • Faster work: AI assistance and automation remove waste.
  • Lower spend: license rationalisation and consumption hygiene.
