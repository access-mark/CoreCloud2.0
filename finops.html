Hybrid FinOps & Governance

Hybrid only works if cost, policy, and operations stay tight. We cut run-rate and stop drift across cloud, hosted, and on-prem with tagging that sticks, budgets and alerts, showback/chargeback, and policy-as-code guardrails.

What You Get in 90 Days

  • 12–35% run-rate reduction: Quick wins on idle/over-provisioned resources, wrong tiers, and forgotten spend.
    • Rightsizing + schedule-off + storage tiering
    • Commitment coverage (RIs/SPs) matched to trend
    • “Turn-down” defaults for dev/test
  • Showback you can defend: Per-workload cost lines that match how execs make decisions.
    • Golden tags with backfill strategy (≥90% coverage)
    • Shared services allocation rules (documented)
  • Guardrails that hold: Budgets, tags, and policies enforced in code across venues — less drift.
    • Preventive controls for sizes/regions/storage classes
    • Budget alerts with variance root-cause workflow
  • Visibility end-to-end: Dashboards for cloud + hosted + on-prem — one truth for finance & tech.
    • Unify usage/cost data sources to a single pane
    • Weekly “cost & SLO” operating rhythm

The FinOps Operating Loop

  • 1) Baseline: Tag audit, account structure, cost sources, shared services, and noisy neighbours.
    • Map apps → projects → cost centers
    • Decide allocation rules for platforms/egress
  • 2) Optimise: Rightsizing, reservations/SPs, storage tiering, K8s/VM allocation, idle cleanup.
    • CPU/memory requests vs actual usage
    • Commitment re-profiling without over-commit
  • 3) Govern: Budgets & alerts, policy-as-code, golden tags, RBAC, preventive controls.
    • Guardrails: size families, regions, backup minimums
    • Exceptions & break-glass rules (documented)
  • 4) Operate: Monthly reviews, anomaly watch, chargeback/showback, SLO tracking.
    • Runbook: variance → action → owner → date
    • Quarterly roadmap refresh with targets

Capabilities That Stick

  • Tagging that survives reality:Golden set, backfill automation, and carve-outs for shared services.
  • Budgets & anomaly detection:Portfolio + workload budgets with alerts, weekly variance reviews, and playbooks.
  • RI/SP & capacity planning:Coverage targets by family/region; commit sizing and timing to avoid shelf-ware.
  • K8s & VM cost allocation:Requests vs usage, namespace/project split, and showback that makes sense.
  • Policy-as-code guardrails:Preventive controls for regions, sizes, storage classes, backup & DR minimums.
  • Unified dashboards:Cloud + hosted + on-prem in one view for finance, platform, and app owners.

Metrics We Track

  • Coverage:Tag coverage %, commitment coverage %, backup/DR coverage.
  • Efficiency:Idle %, rightsized %, storage tier mix, K8s request/utilisation ratio.
  • Financial:Run-rate, budget variance, unit cost per transaction/user/API.
  • Stability:Anomaly MTTR, change-related drift, SLO adherence.

Risks We Reduce

  • Uncontrolled spend:No-tag, wrong-tier, zombie resources, and egress surprises.
  • Operational drift:Non-standard regions/sizes, backup gaps, and policy violations.
  • Over-commit/under-commit:Mismatch between commitments and actual trends.
  • Opaque allocations:“Who owns this?” → clear showback and chargeback.

Artifacts You’ll Have

  • Tagging standard & exceptions:Golden tags, patterns, and break-glass rules.
  • Guardrail policy pack:Preventive controls as code with test cases.
  • Budget model + dashboards:Portfolio/workload budgets, alerts, and views.
  • Savings roadmap (12 months):Sequenced actions with owners and targets.

Built for South Africa

  • POPIA-aligned cost views:Separate residency-sensitive workloads; minimise cross-border egress surprises.
  • Latency & locality economics:Model cost vs jitter for on-prem/hosted placement without losing cloud services.
  • Vendor leverage:Multi-venue posture that improves pricing power with hyperscalers and DC partners.
  • Power & DR reality:Backups you can test, run-books that assume load-shedding and link failover.
