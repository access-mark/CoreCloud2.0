How it works

Phase 1 — Formation

(Pty) Ltd registration; governance, banking, tax, POPIA; B-BBEE roadmap for QSE (R10m–R50m turnover class); brand, domains, site, CRM; GTM plan; initial shareholding with operator vesting.

Phase 2 — Incubation

Operate on CoreCloud shared services; access frameworks, leads, partners; targets: 2–3 recurring clients, path to R5m+ ARR run-rate.

Phase 3 — Buy-Out & Graduation

Trigger when revenue & maturity KPIs met (≥ R10m turnover). Buy-out over 12–18 months via pre-agreed formula (e.g., 2×–3× EBITDA). QSE graduates and remains a Collective partner.
