How it works

Phase 1 — Formation

  • Register (Pty) Ltd, governance, banking, tax & compliance.
  • B‑BBEE plan aligned to QSE requirements (R10m–R50m turnover class).
  • Brand, site, domains, CRM, GTM plan and first offers.
  • Initial shareholding: CoreCloud majority; operator equity vesting.

Phase 2 — Incubation

  • Operate using shared services: Finance, HR, legal, marketing, sales.
  • Access to CoreCloud leads, frameworks and partner vendors.
  • Targets: 2–3 recurring clients and R5m+ annual run‑rate.

Phase 3 — Buy‑Out & Graduation

  • Trigger: revenue passes R10m with maturity KPIs met.
  • Buy‑out via fixed formula (e.g. multiple on EBITDA) over 12–18 months.
  • Graduate as a stand‑alone business and Collective partner.
