Modern Datacenter

The Data Centre — Private Cloud. 60+ years combined CoreCloud experience in DC and private cloud. We recommend best-of-breed data protection and modern compute, networking, security, and storage — in classic 3-2-1 or modern hyper-converged platforms.

Request a Datacenter Assessment

Outcomes We Commit To

  • Predictable run-rate: no surprise egress, clear unit prices, showback by app/team.
  • Low-latency UX: keep critical paths close to users, branches, and plants.
  • Resilience proof: prove-you-can-recover drills, documented RPO/RTO, quarterly failovers.
  • POPIA alignment: data-class rules, residency, key custody, revocation logs.

When Modern DC Beats Hyperscale

  • Steady workloads (ERP, databases, integration hubs) with predictable growth.
  • Residency & sovereignty requirements with board-level reporting.
  • Latency-sensitive flows (trading, manufacturing, contact centres).
  • Complex licensing (DB, middleware) where venue affects TCO materially.

Reference Architectures

Traditional 3-2-1

Hyper-Converged

Scale-out compute+storage fabrics, simplified ops, stretch/metro DR orchestration.

Hybrid Burst

FAQ

Can we keep data in-country and still burst?

Yes. We keep primary data resident and govern analytics/peak bursts to AWS/Azure by data-class rules with showback.

Do you bring hardware or use our DC?

Either. We can supply and manage, or operate your estate under the same guardrails and SLOs.

How do you prove recoverability?

Automated backups, restore tests, and monthly prove-you-can-recover logs mapped to each app.
