Infrastructure — Private or Hybrid Cloud

On-Premise, Co-Locate, Hyperscale or Hybrid — not every workload belongs in a hyperscale platform.

We guide South African enterprises to run stable, regulated, and latency-critical systems on the most cost-effective platform — managed private cloud, co-location, or hybrid.

When cloud is right, we use it. When predictability wins, we host.

Data Governance & Protection — POPIA-aware delivery and industry alignment baked into blueprints, operations, and reporting.

Right Design, Right Location

  • Stable, business-critical workloads: ERP, databases, integration hubs with predictable outcomes.
  • Residency & sovereignty: your data in the right data centre and country — compliant with regulations.
  • Low-latency proximity: keep compute close to users, branches, or plants.
  • Economics: avoid ingress/egress traps, legacy licensing shocks, and over-provisioned capacity.
  • Operational certainty: clear SLAs, tested DR, reliability patterns that hold under pressure.

What Can We Run

  • Single-tenant private cloud (VMs/containers) with dedicated network segments.
  • Core databases (PostgreSQL, SQL Server, Oracle*) with backup/restore drills and prove-you-can-recover logs.
  • ERP and line-of-business apps with integration brokers and secure partner connectivity.
  • Edge and branch workloads with centralised observability and config drift detection.
  • VDI and specialised workloads with GPU options where needed.

*We optimise licensing footprints and placement — venue-neutral recommendations with 12/36-month TCO.

Architecture & Controls

  • Platform: Servers, networking, and storage from leading vendors.
  • Networks: segmented VLANs/VNETs, egress control, private interconnects, and WAF/CDN for public edges.
  • Identity & access: SSO, least-privilege roles, break-glass, and auditable revocation.
  • Data protection: 3-2-1 backups, cyber-resilient storage, and DPA-aligned DR with routine testing.
  • Observability: AI Ops, NOC/SOC with logs, metrics, traces — governed by SLOs and error budgets.
  • Hybrid platforms: burst to AWS/Azure for peak or analytics — with showback.

FAQ

Can we keep data in-country and still use hyperscalers?

Yes. Private cloud can be primary with analytics or burst capacity in AWS/Azure. We design data-class rules so sensitive data stays local while aggregates can move.

Do you supply hardware or use our DC?

Both are possible. We can provide managed private cloud or operate in your chosen DC/colo with the same guardrails and SLOs.

What if we need to move later?

We favour open standards, portable data formats, and documented runbooks so moves between venues remain practical.
