Modern Datacenter

The Data Centre — Private Cloud. 60+ years combined CoreCloud experience in DC and private cloud design. We recommend best-of-breed data protection and modern compute, networking, security, and storage — in classic 3-2-1 or modern Hyper-Converged platforms.

Request a Datacenter Assessment

A Modern Datacentre

  • Multi-venue reality: Run on-prem, in hosted colo, and in public cloud.
  • Right venue, right time: Decisions that grow and adapt with your organisation — avoid long-run cost traps.
  • Balance the triad: Performance, availability, reliability tuned to business goals.

Infrastructure — On Demand

Repatriation is real: customers want hybrid-cloud platforms that bring public-cloud flexibility into the DC with on-demand, dedicated infrastructure. We blend OPEX and CAPEX to enable consumption models — scalable, commercially efficient, and resilient.

Reference Architectures

  • Traditional 3-2-1: Clear RPO/RTO tiers, air-gaps, immutable copies.
  • Hyper-Converged: Scale-out fabrics, simplified ops, DR orchestration.
  • Hybrid Burst: Primary data local; burst to AWS/Azure for analytics or peak with showback.

What We Run

  • Private cloud (VMs/containers), segmented networks, identity guardrails.
  • Databases (PostgreSQL, SQL Server, Oracle) with prove-you-can-recover drills.
  • ERP/LOB stacks, integration brokers, secure partner connectivity.
  • Edge/branch workloads, central observability, config drift detection.
  • VDI and special compute incl. GPU where required.

Service Levels (Typical)

  • Availability: 99.9–99.99%
  • RPO / RTO: ≤15m / ≤60m*
  • Backup verification: Daily jobs + monthly test restores
  • DR/cyber drills: Quarterly
  • Security posture: Monthly board report

*Tighter targets available with sync replication and multi-AZ/DC designs.
