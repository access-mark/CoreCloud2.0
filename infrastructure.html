Modern Datacenter

The Data Centre — Private Cloud

CoreCloud’s 60+ years of combined experience in datacenter and private cloud design and solution sales allows us to recommend best-of-breed data protection and modern technology for compute, networking, security, and data storage — in either traditional 3-2-1 architectures or modern HC (Hyper-Converged) platforms.

A Modern Datacentre

  • Multi-venue reality: Modern workloads run on-prem, in hosted colo, and in public cloud.
  • Right venue, right time: We guide decisions that grow and adapt with your organisation — avoiding long-run cost traps.
  • Balance the triad: Performance, availability, and reliability tuned to your business goals.

Infrastructure — On Demand

Industry trends are shifting: repatriation of workloads is driving demand for hybrid-cloud platforms — bringing the flexibility of public cloud into the datacentre with on-demand, dedicated infrastructure.

We combine OPEX and CAPEX options to support a consumption operating model. You get scalability and commercial efficiency while maintaining best-in-breed availability, continuity, and management for datacentre infrastructure.

Reference Architectures

  • Traditional 3-2-1: Proven backup/restore, clear RPO/RTO tiers, air-gaps and immutable copies.
  • Hyper-Converged: Compute-storage-network fabrics with scale-out growth, simplified ops, and DR orchestration.
  • Hybrid Burst: Keep primary data local; burst to AWS/Azure for analytics or peak — governed by data-class rules and showback.

What We Run

  • Private cloud (VMs/containers) with segmented networks and identity guardrails.
  • Databases (PostgreSQL, SQL Server, Oracle) with prove-you-can-recover drills.
  • ERP/LOB stacks, integration brokers, secure partner connectivity.
  • Edge/branch workloads with central observability and config drift detection.
  • VDI and special compute incl. GPU where required.
