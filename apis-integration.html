Atlas — Your Living Delivery Blueprint

Reference architectures, playbooks, and guardrails your teams actually use—kept current, measurable, and tied to cost, reliability, and speed.

What Atlas Is

Single source of truth: cloud & platform blueprints, data/AI runways, security guardrails, and FinOps policies in one place.

Living, not static: versioned, reviewed monthly, and wired into CI/CD so standards show up where engineers work.

Outcome-led: each pattern maps to an owner, metric, and review rhythm (SLO, run-rate, change lead time).

What’s Inside

  • Cloud foundations: landing zones, identity, networks, backups, DR tiers.
  • App delivery: golden paths for services, portals, mobile/web, APIs & EDA.
  • Data & AI: lakehouse layers, streaming, RAG/MLOps, lineage & privacy.
  • Security & governance: policy-as-code, secrets, access, key custody.
  • FinOps: tags, budgets, anomaly alerts, commitment guardrails.

How It Works

Discover & calibrate: assess current patterns, risk, and run-rate; pick the first golden paths.

Codify & wire-in: templates, modules, and checks land in repos, pipelines, and portals.

Operate & review: monthly WBR for cost/reliability; backlog of next best moves is closed, not studied.
