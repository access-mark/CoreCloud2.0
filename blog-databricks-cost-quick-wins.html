Databricks Cost: Quick Wins

Cloud • 25 Sep 2025 • 4-minute read

Databricks is powerful — but mis-configured, it’s a silent budget drain. These three quick wins save most SA teams 20–30% in month one.

1) Right-size clusters

Most jobs run fine on smaller worker types. Benchmark your biggest notebooks, then drop size by one tier. Savings: 10–15% instantly.

2) Idle termination

Default is 120 minutes. Set auto-termination to 10–15 min. Jobs clusters only. Savings: thousands on forgotten workloads.

3) Spot vs on-demand

Non-prod and batch jobs? Use spot. Mix with on-demand for SLAs. Savings: 60–70% on dev/test cycles.
